From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Tool used for project and business process management

[![Image 5](https://thumb.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/b/bc/RACI.png/250px-RACI.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=parser&utm_content=thumbnail)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:RACI.png)

Example of a responsibility assignment matrix.

A **responsibility assignment matrix**, **RACI matrix** (_responsible, accountable, consulted and informed matrix_; [/ˈ r eɪ s i/](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:IPA/English "Help:IPA/English")) or **linear responsibility chart**, is a [project management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_management "Project management") technique that describes the responsibilities of various stakeholders in completing [tasks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Task_(project_management) "Task (project management)") or [deliverables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deliverable "Deliverable"). The matrix assigns one of four responsibilities to each stakeholder in executing a deliverable: _Responsible_, _Accountable_, _Consulted_, and _Informed_.

Under the RACI framework:

*   [Responsible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duty "Duty") stakeholders are involved in the planning, execution, and completion of the task;
*   [Accountable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accountability "Accountability") stakeholders are held to be individually and ultimately responsible for the success or failure of the task;
*   Consulted stakeholders are sought for their opinions on a task;
*   Informed stakeholders are updated as the project progresses.

## RACI model

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Responsibility_assignment_matrix&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: RACI model")]

RACI is a model that describes the participation of various [roles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Role "Role") in completing [tasks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Task_(project_management) "Task (project management)") or [deliverables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deliverable "Deliverable") for a [project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project "Project") or [business process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_process "Business process").[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-FOOTNOTEProject_Management_Institute2021%C2%A74.6.6_Focus_on_Value-1) The four key responsibilities most typically used are: _responsible_, _accountable_, _consulted_, and _informed_. It is often used to define roles and responsibilities in cross-departmental projects.[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-babok2-2)

_R: Responsible —_ Those who complete the task.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-pmicie-3) There is at least one role with a participation type of _responsible_, although others can be delegated to assist in the work required.

_A: Accountable_ (also _approver_ or _approving authority_) — The one ultimately answerable for the correct completion of the deliverable or task, ensuring the prerequisites of the task are met, and delegating the work to those _responsible_.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-pmicie-3) Accountable stakeholders sign off and approve work that _responsible_ stakeholders provide. According to some theories of project management, there must be only one accountable stakeholder specified for each task or deliverable.[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-formal-4)

_C: Consulted —_ Those whose opinions are sought, such as [subject-matter experts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subject-matter_expert "Subject-matter expert"), and with whom there is two-way communication.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-pmicie-3)

_I: Informed —_ Those who are kept up-to-date on progress, often only on completion of the task or deliverable, and with whom there is just one-way communication.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-pmicie-3)

The RACI model is also known as a responsibility assignment matrix and linear responsibility chart.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-5)[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-pmbok-6)[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-slaguide-7)[[8]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-8)

### Alternatives

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Responsibility_assignment_matrix&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Alternatives")]

There are a number of alternatives to the RACI model, including:

*   DACI: Driver, Approvers, Contributors, Informed[[9]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-9)
*   PARIS: Participant, Accountable, Review required, Input required, Sign-off required[[10]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-10)
*   RACI + F: includes Facilitation in [Scrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrum_(software_development) "Scrum (software development)") projects[[11]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-Archived_copy-11)
*   RACIS/RASCI/RASIC/RASI: includes Support[[12]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-12)[[13]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-13)
*   RACIO/CAIRO: includes Omitted (or Out-of-the-loop)[[14]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-14)[[15]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-15)
*   RACIQ: includes Quality review
*   RACI-VSL includes Verifier and Signatory[[7]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Responsibility_assignment_matrix#cite_note-slaguide-7)

## Charting

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Responsibility_assignment_matrix&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Charting")]

Responsibility assignment matrices are charted with the vertical axis representing tasks or deliverables and the horizontal axis representing roles.

Example RACI chart| Tasks | Maintenance supervisors | Maintenance analyst | Maintenance planner | Maintenance technician | Maintenance support | Rel specialist | CMMS project engineer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Inputting failure data | A | C | I | R |  | C | C |
| Work order completion | R | C | C | C | A | I | I |
| Work order closeout | C | R | C |  | I | I | A |
| QA of failure data input | C | R | I | C | I | C | A |
| Analyze failure reports | C | C | I | C | A | R | I |
| Maintenance strategy adjustments | C | I | I | C | A | R | R |
| Implementing new strategies | R | I | R | C | A | I | I |
