The [Malay language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language "Malay language") has a complex system of [styles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Style_(manner_of_address) "Style (manner of address)"), [titles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Title "Title") and [honorifics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorific "Honorific") which are used extensively in [Brunei Darussalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunei "Brunei"), [Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysia "Malaysia") and [Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore "Singapore").

Brunei, Malaysia, Singapore, few provinces in the Philippines and several provinces in Indonesia regularly award honorary and life titles.[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation\_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_] What follows in this article is specific to the Malaysian system. References to Brunei and Indonesia are given when pertinent.

In Malaysia, all non-[hereditary titles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hereditary_title "Hereditary title") can be granted to both men and women. Every title has a [form of address](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Form_of_address "Form of address") which can be used by the wife of the title holder. This form is not used by the husband of a titled woman; such a woman will bear a title which is the same as a titled man.[_[citation needed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Citation\_needed "Wikipedia:Citation needed")_]

## Former usage

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: Former usage")]

[Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore "Singapore"), whose Malay royalty was abolished by the [British colonial government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_in_the_Straits_Settlements "Singapore in the Straits Settlements") in 1891, has adopted civic titles for its leaders. However, when the officials and leaders who were bestowed the Malay titles by Malaysia and Brunei, the title will be only referred to on their visit to the title's state's local media.

Much of the [Philippines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philippines "Philippines") was [historically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Philippines_(900%E2%80%931521) "History of the Philippines (900–1521)") accustomed to the usage of Malay titles by [its royals and nobles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principalia "Principalia"), such as [Raja Sulayman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raja_Sulayman "Raja Sulayman") and [Dayang Kalangitan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dayang_Kalangitan "Dayang Kalangitan") in Luzon. Malay titles are still prominently used by the royal houses of [Sulu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Sulu "Sultanate of Sulu")[[1]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-Malay_Muslims-1)[[2]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-sultanateofsulu-2) and [Maguindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultanate_of_Maguindanao "Sultanate of Maguindanao") among other noble lineages in [Mindanao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindanao "Mindanao") in preservation of genealogies. Officially, the republic does not grant royal or noble titles according to the [1987 Constitution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1987_Constitution "1987 Constitution").

[Indonesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indonesia "Indonesia"), meanwhile, as a republic, does not recognise hereditary rulers and aristocratic systems outside of [Yogyakarta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yogyakarta_Sultanate "Yogyakarta Sultanate").

## Usage

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Usage")]

The sequence that should be used when formally writing or addressing a person's name is: honorary style, professional rank, royal hereditary title, federal title, state title, non-royal hereditary title, Doctor (of medicine or philosophy), Haji/Hajah (for [Muslim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muslim "Muslim") men and women who have performed the [Hajj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hajj "Hajj")), name.

For instance, in Brunei, the [Wazir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vizier_(Brunei) "Vizier (Brunei)") is a group of royal nobles, namely one of the royal princes who is _gahara_ (pure descendants of the [Sultan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_of_Brunei "Sultan of Brunei")), are the second-highest official post in the nation, right behind the Sultan. Following this, the [Cheteria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cheteria "Cheteria"), only bestowed upon the [Pengiran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pengiran "Pengiran"), who perform specific jobs and are ranked above the [Manteri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manteri "Manteri"), a group of non-royal nobles.[[3]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-:0-3) An example of a Manteri would be the former [police commissioner of Brunei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Brunei_Police_Force "Royal Brunei Police Force"), [Hasrin Sabtu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasrin_Sabtu "Hasrin Sabtu"), whose honorary title would be _Yang Dimuliakan_ ([The Exalted One](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Excellency "Excellency")), noble rank is _Pehin Orang Kaya Pendikar Alam_, state title is _[Dato Paduka Seri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order\_of\_Paduka\_Keberanian\_Laila\_Terbilang "Order of Paduka Keberanian Laila Terbilang")_ and traditional Bruneian prefix title for non-royalty is _Awang_ (Mr.).[[4]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-4)

When in the home state, the state title may precede the [federal title](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders,_decorations,_and_medals_of_Malaysia#Federal_orders,_decorations,_and_medals "Orders, decorations, and medals of Malaysia"). An example is the current [Premier of Sarawak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_of_Sarawak "Premier of Sarawak"), [Abang Abdul Rahman Zohari Abang Openg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abang_Abdul_Rahman_Zohari_Abang_Openg "Abang Abdul Rahman Zohari Abang Openg"), whose federal title is _Tan Sri_, whose state title is _Datuk Patinggi_ and Bruniean title is _Dato Seri Setia_. His title will be expressed as either:

*   _Yang Amat Berhormat Tan Sri Datuk Patinggi_ (Dr.) Abang Haji Abdul Rahman Zohari bin Tun Datuk Abang Haji Openg (federally)
*   _Yang Amat Berhormat Datuk Patinggi Tan Sri_ (Dr.) Abang Haji Abdul Rahman Zohari bin Tun Datuk Abang Haji Openg (in his home state).
*   _Yang Amat Berhormat Tan Sri Dato Seri Setia_ (Dr.) Abang Haji Abdul Rahman Zohari bin Tun Datuk Abang Haji Openg (in [Brunei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunei "Brunei")).

Another exception is when a person has received an award from a state other than the person's home state; when visiting the award-bestowing state, that state's title will take the place of a home state's title (if any). As an example, the current Prime Minister, [Anwar Ibrahim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anwar_Ibrahim "Anwar Ibrahim") will be titled as follows:

*   _Yang Amat Berhormat Dato' Seri_ Anwar bin Ibrahim (federally)
*   _Yang Amat Berhormat Dato'_ Anwar bin Ibrahim (in [Kelantan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelantan "Kelantan"))
*   _Yang Amat Berhormat Dato' Seri Utama_ Anwar bin Ibrahim (in [Penang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penang "Penang"))
*   _Yang Amat Berhormat Dato' Seri Diraja_ Anwar bin Ibrahim (in [Perlis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perlis "Perlis"))
*   _Yang Amat Berhormat Datuk Seri Panglima_ Anwar bin Ibrahim (in [Sabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabah "Sabah"))
*   _Yang Amat Berhormat Dato Laila Utama_ Anwar bin Ibrahim (in [Brunei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunei "Brunei"))

A style carried by virtue of royal title always trumps those carried by non-royal titles. Male royals may choose to append 'al-Haj' to their name instead of using 'Haji'. The following example is correct:

*   _Yang Amat Mulia_ General Tengku Dato' (name) al-Haj.

## Malay royalty

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section: Malay royalty")]

The following titles are hereditary and reserved for the royal families of [Brunei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunei "Brunei") and nine royal states of [Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysia "Malaysia").

### Brunei

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Brunei")]

According to royal customs established during the reign of [Sultan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_of_Brunei "Sultan of Brunei")[Hassanal Bolkiah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hassanal_Bolkiah "Hassanal Bolkiah"), and based on knowledge passed down by elders, any name, rank, title, position, or honorific must be preceded by a _terasul_ before being mentioned or written. This tradition reflects the deep respect for hierarchical and cultural values in Brunei. Given that Brunei is an absolute monarchy, the correct use of official titles for members of the royal family is essential.[[5]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-FOOTNOTEMohd._Jamil_Al-Sufri20036-5) Incorrect usage can cause discomfort or be perceived as disrespectful, emphasising the importance of adhering to these formal protocols.[[6]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-6)

*   Before reaching puberty: _Kebawah Duli Sultan_ (followed by the Sultan's given name).
*   Before the [coronation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coronation "Coronation"): _Kebawah Duli Yang Maha Mulia Paduka Seri Sultan_ (followed by the Sultan's given name).
*   After the coronation: _Kebawah Duli Yang Maha Mulia Paduka Seri Baginda Sultan_ (followed by the Sultan's given name), Sultan and Yang Di-Pertuan.
*   After the coronation and performing the [Hajj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hajj "Hajj"): _Kebawah Duli Yang Maha Mulia Paduka Seri Baginda Sultan Haji_[[a]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-7) (followed by the Sultan's given name), Sultan and Yang Di-Pertuan.
*   Upon [abdication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdication "Abdication"): _Duli Yang Teramat Mulia Paduka Seri Begawan Sultan_ (followed by the Sultan's given name).
*   Upon abdication after performing the Hajj: _Duli Yang Teramat Mulia Paduka Seri Begawan Sultan Haji_[[b]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-8) (followed by the Sultan's given name).

*   Sultan's wife from [common lineage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commoner "Commoner"):[[f]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-13)_Duli Yang Teramat Mulia Pengiran Isteri_ or _Pengiran Bini_[[g]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-14) (followed by her given name).

### Malaysia

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=5 "Edit section: Malaysia")]

*   _Kebawah Duli Yang Maha Mulia_ (KDYMM) (literally 'He/She who is below the dust of The Almighty') is used for the Yang di-Pertuan Agong, and state rulers alike. The title is a reference to the rulers being subjected to the Law of God with their powers below that of [Allah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allah "Allah"). However, the Yang di-Pertuan Agong also uses the prefix 'Seri Paduka Baginda' (literally, 'He, the Footwear of the Prophet') and in English, his title is often translated as 'His Majesty'. The title is a reference to the rulers being in comparison to the prophet [Muhammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad "Muhammad"), where the word _[paduka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paduka "Paduka")_ is translated in [Malay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_language "Malay language") as a type of footwear. In this styling, the Supreme Ruler is only the level of footwear in comparison to the Prophet. However, the style differs from state to state as according to states' tradition.

#### Federal

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Federal")]

Titles of Malaysian royalty and rulers:

*   [Yang di-Pertuan Agong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang_di-Pertuan_Agong "Yang di-Pertuan Agong") (literally, 'He who is made Supreme Lord' but usually 'Supreme Head' or 'Paramount Ruler') is the official title of the ruler of all Malaysia, elected from among the nine heads of the royal families. The title is often glossed 'King' in English. He is styled as _Kebawah Duli Yang Maha Mulia Seri Paduka Baginda_ (His Majesty).
*   [Timbalan Yang di-Pertuan Agong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deputy_Yang_di-Pertuan_Agong "Deputy Yang di-Pertuan Agong") (literally, 'Deputy of He who is made Supreme Lord', but usually 'Deputy Supreme Head' or 'Deputy Paramount Ruler') is the official title of the deputy ruler of all Malaysia who is also elected from among the nine heads of the royal families. The title is often glossed 'Deputy King' in English. He is styled as _Kebawah Duli Yang Maha Mulia_ (His Royal Highness).
*   [Raja Permaisuri Agong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raja_Permaisuri_Agong "Raja Permaisuri Agong") (literally, 'The Supreme Lady') is the official title of the consort of the ruler of Malaysia. The title is often glossed 'Queen' in English. She is styled as _Kebawah Duli Yang Maha Mulia Seri Paduka Baginda_ (Her Majesty).
*   [Yang di-Pertua Negeri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang_di-Pertua_Negeri "Yang di-Pertua Negeri") (YDPN) is not a royal title, but the title of 'The Head of the State' (the 'Supreme Head') for the state of [Penang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penang "Penang"), [Melaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melaka "Melaka"), [Sabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabah "Sabah"), and [Sarawak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarawak "Sarawak"), which do not have hereditary rulers. Yang di-Pertua Negeri is installed by the [Yang di-Pertuan Agong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang_di-Pertuan_Agong "Yang di-Pertuan Agong"). The title is sometimes translated as 'Governor' in English. They are styled _Tuan/Puan Yang Terutama_ (His/Her Excellency).

#### Negeri Sembilan

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=7 "Edit section: Negeri Sembilan")]

*   [Yamtuan Besar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamtuan_Besar "Yamtuan Besar"), officially [Yang di-Pertuan Besar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang_di-Pertuan_Besar "Yang di-Pertuan Besar") (literally 'He who is made Chief Ruler',) is the title of the ruler of [Negeri Sembilan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Negeri_Sembilan "Negeri Sembilan"). He is styled as _Duli Yang Maha Mulia_ (His Royal Highness).
*   _Tunku Ampuan Besar_ ('The Chief Royal Consort') is the title of the queen consort of royal parentage for Negeri Sembilan. She is styled as _Duli Yang Maha Mulia_ (Her Royal Highness).
*   _Che' Ampuan Besar_ is the title of the queen consort of non-royal blood (commoner). She is styled as _Duli Yang Teramat Mulia_ (Her Royal Highness).
*   _Tunku Ampuan_ ('The Chief Dowager') is the title of the queen [dowager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dowager "Dowager"). She is styled as _Yang Maha Mulia_ (Her Royal Highness).
*   _Tunku Puan Besar_ ('The Senior Chief Dowager') is the title of the senior queen dowager. She is styled _Yang Maha Mulia_ (Her Royal Highness).
*   The children of rulers have the title and style of _Yang Amat Mulia_ (His/Her Highness) Tunku.
*   The fiefs of the state, the _[Undangs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undangs "Undangs")_ are for the areas (_luak_) of [Jelebu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jelebu "Jelebu"), [Johol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johol "Johol"), [Sungai Ujong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sungai_Ujong "Sungai Ujong"), and [Rembau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rembau_District "Rembau District"). ## Federal titles

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=16 "Edit section: Federal titles")]

In Malaysia, the _[Yang di-Pertuan Agong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang\_di-Pertuan\_Agong "Yang di-Pertuan Agong")_ (King of Malaysia) grants honours to recipients nominated by the [Government of Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Malaysia "Government of Malaysia") as awards which are honorary and non-hereditary. These honours may also be revoked by the _Yang di-Pertuan Agong_ or returned by the individual. Some of the highest honours bestowed carries with them the titles of _Tun_, _Tan Sri_, or _Datuk_.

There is a maximum number of Malaysian subjects who may be award-holders at any one time. These numerical limits apply only to Malaysian subjects. Foreigners may be awarded such titles in a supernumerary and honorary capacity, and may use the title locally.

### Tun

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=17 "Edit section: Tun")]

The _Tun_ title has existed in Malay traditional society for hundreds of years. In ancient times, _Tun_ was an honorific title used by noble people of royal lineage, inherited by the male descendants. Over time, the _Tun_ title has become a title conferred by the Yang di-Pertuan Agong to the most-deserving recipient who has highly contributed to the nation.

_Tun_ is the most senior federal title awarded to recipients of either the _Seri Maharaja Mangku Negara_ ([Grand Commander of the Order of the Defender of the Realm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Defender_of_the_Realm#Grand_Commander "Order of the Defender of the Realm")) (SMN) or _Seri Setia Mahkota_ ([Grand Commander of the Order of Loyalty to the Crown of Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Loyalty_to_the_Crown_of_Malaysia#Grand_Commander "Order of Loyalty to the Crown of Malaysia")) (SSM). However, the SMN and SSM are [not the highest federal awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders,_decorations,_and_medals_of_Malaysia#Federal_orders,_decorations,_and_medals "Orders, decorations, and medals of Malaysia"), ranking fourth and fifth, respectively. There may not be more than 25 (SMN) and 35 (SSM) living holders of each of these awards respectively at any one time. The title for the wife of a _Tun_ is called _Toh Puan_. The recipients and their wives are then bestowed with the style of _Yang Amat Berbahagia_ or (The Most Honourable).

The SMN is usually awarded to newly appointed _Yang di-Pertua Negeri_ (YDPN), and to all the retired [Prime Ministers of Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Ministers_of_Malaysia "Prime Ministers of Malaysia"), with the exception of [Tunku Abdul Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunku_Abdul_Rahman "Tunku Abdul Rahman") (he is the [Prince](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince_of_Kedah?action=edit&redlink=1 "Prince of Kedah (page does not exist)") of the state of [Kedah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kedah "Kedah")), [Najib Razak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Najib_Razak "Najib Razak"), [Muhyiddin Yassin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhyiddin_Yassin "Muhyiddin Yassin"), and [Ismail Sabri Yaakob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ismail_Sabri_Yaakob "Ismail Sabri Yaakob").

Examples:

*   _Yang Amat Berbahagia_ (The Most Honourable) _Tun_[Dr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physician "Physician")[Mahathir Mohamad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahathir_Mohamad "Mahathir Mohamad")[SMN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Defender_of_the_Realm#Grand_Commander "Order of the Defender of the Realm");
*   _Yang Amat Berbahagia_ (The Most Honourable) _Tun_[Dr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr. "Dr.")[Ling Liong Sik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ling_Liong_Sik "Ling Liong Sik")[SSM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Loyalty_to_the_Crown_of_Malaysia "Order of Loyalty to the Crown of Malaysia").

### Tan Sri

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=18 "Edit section: Tan Sri")]

_Tan Sri_ is the second-most senior federal title and a honorific, used to denote recipients of the _Panglima Mangku Negara_ ([Commander of the Order of the Defender of the Realm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Defender_of_the_Realm#Commander "Order of the Defender of the Realm")) (PMN) and the _Panglima Setia Mahkota_ ([Commander of the Order of Loyalty to the Crown of Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Loyalty_to_the_Crown_of_Malaysia#Commander "Order of Loyalty to the Crown of Malaysia")) (PSM). The wife of a _Tan Sri_ is called _Puan Sri_. The recipients and their wives are then bestowed with the style of _Yang Berbahagia_ or (The Honourable).

Examples:

*   _Tan Sri Datuk Amar_[P. Ramlee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._Ramlee "P. Ramlee")
*   _Yang Berhormat Tan Sri Dato' Haji_[Muhyiddin Yassin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhyiddin_Yassin "Muhyiddin Yassin")[PSM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Loyalty_to_the_Crown_of_Malaysia#Commander "Order of Loyalty to the Crown of Malaysia");
*   _Yang Berbahagia Tan Sri Datuk Seri Panglima_[Abdul Gani Patail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Gani_Patail "Abdul Gani Patail")[PMN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Defender_of_the_Realm#Commander "Order of the Defender of the Realm");
*   _Yang Berbahagia Tan Sri_ Dr [Runme Shaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runme_Shaw "Runme Shaw"), [K.St.J](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Saint_John_(chartered_1888) "Order of Saint John (chartered 1888)");
*   _Yang Berbahagia Tan Sri Dato' Seri_[Michelle Yeoh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Yeoh "Michelle Yeoh")[PSM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Loyalty_to_the_Crown_of_Malaysia#Commander "Order of Loyalty to the Crown of Malaysia");
*   _Yang Berbahagia Tan Sri Dato' Sri_ Dr [Mohamed Shafee Abdullah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamed_Shafee_Abdullah "Mohamed Shafee Abdullah")[PSM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Loyalty_to_the_Crown_of_Malaysia "Order of Loyalty to the Crown of Malaysia").

### Datuk

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=19 "Edit section: Datuk")]

Main article: [Datuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Datuk "Datuk")

_Datuk_ is a federal title that has been conferred since [1965](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1965_in_Malaysia "1965 in Malaysia"). It is limited to recipients of _Panglima Jasa Negara_ ([Commander of the Order of Meritorious Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Meritorious_Service#Commander "Order of Meritorious Service")) (PJN) and _Panglima Setia Diraja_ ([Commander of the Order of Loyalty to the Royal Family of Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Loyalty_to_the_Royal_Family_of_Malaysia#Commander "Order of Loyalty to the Royal Family of Malaysia")) (PSD). There may be up to 200 living PJN holders and 200 living PSD holders at any one time. The title of the wife of a male Datuk is _Datin_; women with the title can take either the title _Datin_ or _Datuk_. The recipients and their wives are then bestowed with the style of _Yang Berbahagia_ (The Honourable).

Examples:

*   _Yang Berbahagia Datuk_[Lee Chong Wei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lee_Chong_Wei "Lee Chong Wei")[PJN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Meritorious_Service#Commander "Order of Meritorious Service");
*   _Yang Berbahagia Datuk_[Punch Gunalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punch_Gunalan "Punch Gunalan")[PSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Loyalty_to_the_Royal_Family_of_Malaysia#Commander "Order of Loyalty to the Royal Family of Malaysia");
*   _Yang Berbahagia Datuk_[Ali Abdul Kadir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ali_Abdul_Kadir?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ali Abdul Kadir (page does not exist)");
*   _Yang Berbahagia Datuk_[Ahmad Fauzi Hasan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmad_Fauzi_Hasan?action=edit&redlink=1 "Ahmad Fauzi Hasan (page does not exist)");
*   _Yang Berbahagia Datuk_ Prof. Dr. [Mohamad Akram Laldin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohamad_Akram_Laldin?action=edit&redlink=1 "Mohamad Akram Laldin (page does not exist)").

### Dato

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=20 "Edit section: Dato")]

In Brunei, the title _Dato_ is now comparable to the [Commonwealth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commonwealth_of_Nations "Commonwealth of Nations")'s [Sir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir "Sir") and is frequently used with a great non-royal chief.[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-:1-40)

## State titles

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=21 "Edit section: State titles")]

In Malaysia, state honours are awarded by the respective heads of the [thirteen states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_Malaysia "States of Malaysia"). Such titles are honorary and non-hereditary. State titles may be revoked or suspended by the head of state, or may be returned by the individual. Dato' Sri or Dato' Seri (sometimes Dato’ is spelled and pronounced Datuk in some states) is the highest state title conferred by the Ruler on the most deserving recipients who have contributed greatly to the nation or state. It ranks below the federal title Tun, and is an honour equivalent to federal title Tan Sri. The wife of a recipient is Datin Sri or Datin Seri. In many cases, the number of Malaysian nationals who may hold a state title or honour at one time is limited. Such limits do not apply to foreigners.

### Pehin

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=22 "Edit section: Pehin")]

This title is mainly used in Brunei Darussalam. An example of the title in Brunei would be _Pehin Orang Kaya Laila Setia Bakti Di-Raja Dato Laila Utama_ Haji Awang [Isa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isa_bin_Ibrahim "Isa bin Ibrahim"), the former Minister of Home Affairs and the current Special Adviser to the Sultan of Brunei.[[18]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-41) The titles refers to the [traditional ministers posts in Brunei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manteri "Manteri").[[17]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-:1-40)

### Pehin Sri

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=23 "Edit section: Pehin Sri")]

This title is mainly used in [Sarawak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarawak "Sarawak"). Awarded to individuals who have been appointed as Yang di-Pertua Negeri of Sarawak or equivalent or higher. An example of the title in Sarawak would be Tun Pehin Sri Dr. Haji [Wan Junaidi Tuanku Jaafar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wan_Junaidi_Tuanku_Jaafar "Wan Junaidi Tuanku Jaafar"), Yang di-Pertua Negeri of Sarawak.

### Seri Setia

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=24 "Edit section: Seri Setia")]

This title is mainly used in [Malacca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malacca "Malacca"). Awarded to individuals who have been appointed as Yang di-Pertua Negeri of Malacca or equivalent or higher. An example of the title in Malacca would be Tun Seri Setia (Dr.) Haji [Mohd Ali Rustam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohd_Ali_Rustam "Mohd Ali Rustam"), Yang di-Pertua Negeri of Malacca.

### JP

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=25 "Edit section: JP")]

[Justice of Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_of_the_Peace#Malaysia "Justice of the Peace") (JP) ranks below all _Dato'_ or _Datuk_. In Malaysia, Justices of Peace have largely been replaced in magistrates' courts by legally-qualified (first-class) stipendiary [magistrates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magistrate "Magistrate"). However, state governments continue to appoint Justices of Peace as honours. In [2004](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_in_Malaysia "2004 in Malaysia"), some associations of JPs pressed the federal government to allow JPs to sit as second-class magistrates to reduce the backlog of cases in the courts.

## Special cases

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=26 "Edit section: Special cases")]

If a person has been awarded several honours from different states, the title used varies. For example, sometimes former Prime Minister, [Mahathir Mohamad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahathir_Mohamad "Mahathir Mohamad") is usually referred to as _YABhg Tun Dr. Mahathir Mohamad_ anywhere in Malaysia. However, different terms of address _may_ be used in the states that he visits. In Sarawak, he will be referred to as _YABhg Tun Pehin Sri Mahathir Mohamad_ as he received the [Knight Grand Commander of the Order of the Star of Sarawak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Most_Exalted_Order_of_the_Star_of_Sarawak#Knight_Grand_Commander "Most Exalted Order of the Star of Sarawak") (SBS) from the [Yang di-Pertua Negeri of Sarawak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang_di-Pertua_Negeri_of_Sarawak "Yang di-Pertua Negeri of Sarawak"). While in Sabah, he is referred to as _Tun Datuk Seri Panglima Dr. Mahathir Mohamad_, as he received the [Grand Commander of the Order of Kinabalu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Kinabalu#Grand_Commander "Order of Kinabalu") (SPDK) from the [Yang di-Pertua Negeri of Sabah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yang_di-Pertua_Negeri_of_Sabah "Yang di-Pertua Negeri of Sabah").

## Honorary styles

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=27 "Edit section: Honorary styles")]

The following are used as styles, both before a person's title, and by themselves as forms of address:

*   _Tuan Yang Terutama_ (TYT) (lit.'The Most Eminent Master') – the style of a state governor, equivalent to 'Your/His Excellency', and also as a title for serving Ambassadors to Malaysia, e.g. T.Y.T. Tuan [Brian D. McFeeters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_D._McFeeters "Brian D. McFeeters"). Previously, the archaic equivalent to the style _His Excellency_. This was used by Singapore, Indonesia, and in Malaysia; for other Republic's leaders, while the style for Governors / Yang di-Pertuas were Tuan _Yang Terutama_.
*   _Yang Amat Berhormat_ (YAB) (lit.'The Right Honourable') – the style of the prime minister, the deputy prime minister, the [Premier of Sarawak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Premier_of_Sarawak "Premier of Sarawak"), the chief ministers and the [Menteri Besars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Ministers_in_Malaysia "Chief Ministers in Malaysia") of the states, and Tuns who are members of parliament. It used to be 'the Right Honourable', but since Malaysia withdrew as a member of the [Privy Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judicial_Committee_of_the_Privy_Council "Judicial Committee of the Privy Council") in 1985, the right to use 'Right Honourable' has been revoked.
*   _Yang Berhormat_ (YB) (lit.'The Honourable') – the style of members of parliament and state legislative assemblymen. The prefix _Yang Berhormat_ is also used for recipients of the First and Second Classes of the [Johor's Orders of Chivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_post-nominal_letters_(Johor) "List of post-nominal letters (Johor)") who is titled Dato' for men and Datin Paduka for women, regardless of whether a member of parliament or not.
*   _Yang Berhormat Mulia_ (YBM) (lit.'His/Her Noble Honour') – the style for a member of royalty who is also a member of parliament (e.g. Yang Berhormat Mulia [Tengku Zulpuri Shah Raja Puji](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tengku_Zulpuri_Shah_Raja_Puji "Tengku Zulpuri Shah Raja Puji"), the MP for [Raub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raub_(federal_constituency) "Raub (federal constituency)")). This style is also used by royals with high ranking federal titles such as Tun (eg. Yang Berhormat Mulia Tun [Raja Uda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raja_Uda "Raja Uda")).
*   _Yang Amat Arif_ (YAA) (lit.'The Very Wise') – the style of the [Chief Justice of Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Justice_of_Malaysia "Chief Justice of Malaysia"), the [President of the Court of Appeal of Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_Court_of_Appeal_of_Malaysia "President of the Court of Appeal of Malaysia"), the [Chief Judge of Malaya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Judge_of_Malaya "Chief Judge of Malaya"), and the [Chief Judge of Sabah and Sarawak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Judge_of_Sabah_and_Sarawak "Chief Judge of Sabah and Sarawak").
*   _Yang Arif_ (YA) (lit.'The Wise') – the style of a judge of the Federal Court or Court of Appeal, as well a judicial commissioner or judge of the High Court of Malaya or the High Court of Sabah and Sarawak. It does not apply to session court judges or magistrates.
*   _Yang Amat Berbahagia_ (YABhg) (lit.'The Most Honourable') – the style of persons with the titles _Tun_ or _Toh Puan_, and the spouses of state governors, the spouse of the prime minister, the spouse of the deputy prime minister, as well as the spouses of state chief ministers.
*   _Yang Berbahagia_ (YBhg) (lit.'The Honourable') (and variants thereof) – the styles of persons with a chivalrous title.
*   _Yang Hormat_ (YHmt) (The Honourable) – the style for recipients of the First and Second Classes of the [Pahang's Orders of Chivalry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders,_decorations,_and_medals_of_the_Malaysian_states_and_federal_territories#Pahang "Orders, decorations, and medals of the Malaysian states and federal territories").
*   Yang Dihormati (YDhmti) (The Respected) – the wife of a recipient of the Johor State Order of Merit conferred by the Sultan of Johor, whose husband is titled The Honourable Dato', The Respected Datin wife's name with Dato' husband's name or Dato' husband's [surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname "Surname") after the wife's name, for example.

The English versions of these styles follow Commonwealth usage. Thus the prime minister, cabinet ministers, senators, state executive councillors and judges of the High Court and above are styled the [Honourable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honourable "Honourable") or the [Right Honourable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_Honourable "Right Honourable").

## Noble family hereditary titles

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=28 "Edit section: Noble family hereditary titles")]

**Malay noble titles** are **family hereditary titles** which signify an individual’s social rank, lineage, and connection to [ruling families](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monarchies_of_Malaysia "Monarchies of Malaysia") within Malay society. Socially, they function as markers of identity and precedence as these titles are naturally used by Malay royal and aristocratic families. Bearers of the royal title **Raja/Tengku/Tunku/Engku/Ungku/Megat/Puteri/Syed/Sharifah** are formally addressed as **His/Her Highness** which is put before the respective hereditary title.

legally, the rights to use these titles are regulated by the Malaysian government and restricted to only individuals within the families. A title is put before a name (His/Her Highness + Title + Given name) eg., _His Highness Tengku Iskandar_.

Inheritance follows a paternal mechanism, whereby titles are transmitted through the male line. Accordingly, a marriage between a titled female and a non-titled male may result in their children bearing a lower title or cease to be passed down depending on the rank of the female's title.

**Royal titles:**

*   **Raja**, (means king) royal title inherited by the children (male and female) of a royal father with the title Raja used by members of the [Perak Royal Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_of_Perak "Sultan of Perak"). _Engku_ is often used to politely address senior members of the Perak Royal Family.
*   **Megat**, royal title inherited by the sons of a royal female titled **Raja** who marries a commoner husband. This title is used by [Perak royalties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_of_Perak "Sultan of Perak") indicating half-blood royalty. The sons of a Megat have the right to inherit this title. However, daughters of a Megat and also daughters of a female Raja who marries a commoner bear the title Puteri:[[19]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-42)
    *   **Puteri**, (means Princess) female version title equivalent to Megat. Inherited by the daughters of a royal female titled **Raja** who marries a commoner husband indicating a Half-blood Royal. Additionally, the daughters of a Megat will also inherit the title Puteri. However, if a Puteri marries a commoner, their children will not bear nor inherit any title.

*   **Tunku**, **Tengku**, (derived from _Tuanku_) royal title inherited through a royal father with such title used by members of the [Johor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_of_Johor "Sultan of Johor"), [Pahang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_of_Pahang "Sultan of Pahang"), [Terengganu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_of_Terengganu "Sultan of Terengganu"), [Selangor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_of_Selangor "Sultan of Selangor"), [Kelantan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_of_Kelantan "Sultan of Kelantan"), [Negeri Sembilan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamtuan_Besar "Yamtuan Besar") and [Kedah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_of_Kedah "Sultan of Kedah") royal families.
*   **Engku**, **Ungku**, royal title inherited (non ruling royal house) through a royal father with such title used by members of the [Terengganu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terengganu "Terengganu"), [Pahang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pahang "Pahang") and [Johor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johor "Johor").
*   **Pengiran**, royal title equivalent to Raja/Tengku/Tunku, inherited through a royal father and used by members of [Brunei Royal Family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sultan_of_Brunei "Sultan of Brunei").

**Noble titles:**

*   **[Syed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sayyid "Sayyid")**, is a title inherited by male descendants, through the male line, from the Islamic prophet [Muhammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad "Muhammad") via his grandsons [Hassan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasan_ibn_Ali "Hasan ibn Ali") and [Hussein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Husayn_ibn_Ali "Husayn ibn Ali"). This title is also used by the members of Perlis Royal Family, [House of Jamullail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Jamalullail_(Perlis) "House of Jamalullail (Perlis)"), tracing the House's lineage back to Prophet Muhammad. 
    *   **Sharifah**, is the female version of Syed, inherited by female descendants of a Syed. 
        *   **Meor** is a title inherited by the male children of a Sharifah with a non-Syed husband.

*   **Wan**, (derived from Pahlawan which means commander/warrior) historically a title granted to high-ranking nobilities _eg., army chiefs, ministers,_ during the 16th century in Kedah, Pahang and some parts of Kelantan. Some families are able to retain this title until today, indicating their distant noble lineage. This title is inherited by the children (male and female) of a male Wan. The children of a female Wan and a non-Wan husband will inherit Che.
*   **[Nik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nik_Family_of_Kelantan "Nik Family of Kelantan")**, historically a title used only by Kelanatanese high-ranking nobilities and some royalties in the 16th century Kelantan. Modern day Kelantanese royalties have changed their title into _Tengku_ since the 18th century. The Nik family of Kelantan still retains this title in their names. This title is inherited by the children (male and female) of male Nik. The children of a female Nik and a non-Nik husband will inherit Wan.

## Other titles

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=29 "Edit section: Other titles")]

*   _Haji_ (or _Hajah_ for female) can be used by people who have completed the [Hajj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hajj "Hajj"). This title is abbreviated as 'Hj.' or 'Hjh.'.
*   _Tuan_ literally means 'master'. Due to its colonial overtones, this term is mostly obsolete. In some states like [Kelantan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelantan "Kelantan"), [Pattani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patani_(historical_region) "Patani (historical region)") means [Prince](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prince "Prince") / [Princess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess "Princess"). A hereditary (paternal) title from one of the lineages of the Royal Family. In addition Tuan as an equivalent of [Sir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir "Sir"), it is used in formal correspondence. When addressing an audience, the plural form 'tuan-tuan' (gentlemen) is used, usually combined as 'tuan-tuan dan puan-puan' (gentlemen and ladies).
*   _Encik_ (abbreviated 'En.') is equivalent to [Mr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr. "Mr."), and can be used by all men. Warrant Officers in the [Singapore Armed Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Armed_Forces "Singapore Armed Forces") are also referred to as _Encik_ informally.
*   _Puan_ (abbreviated 'Pn.') can be used by all married women. It is equivalent to [Madam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madam "Madam"), not [Mrs.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mrs. "Mrs."), as most married women in Malay-speaking countries do not use the names or surnames of their husbands. For married women who use their husbands' names, they can be addressed as _Puan (husband's name)_. It is also used in formal correspondence. When addressing an audience, the plural form 'puan-puan' (ladies) is used, usually combined with 'tuan-tuan' as 'tuan-tuan dan puan-puan'.
*   _Cik_ is equivalent to [Miss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miss "Miss"), and can be used by all unmarried women.

## Related issues

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=30 "Edit section: Related issues")]

Not all _Datuk_ s have lived exemplary lives, and some have been convicted of crimes. The various rulers have recently taken steps to ensure the integrity of the institution by means of consultation and the revoking of the given titles.

[Mahathir Mohamad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahathir_Mohamad "Mahathir Mohamad") mentioned that one of the problems with titles in Malaysia is the numbers of them given out. He stated in an interview "Personally, I feel if you want to give value to anything, it must be limited... if you produce a million Ferrari cars, nobody will care about buying a Ferrari."[[20]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-43)

The Sultan [Nazrin Shah of Perak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazrin_Shah_of_Perak "Nazrin Shah of Perak"), stated "That is my view. You degrade the award and the Ruler has the right to revoke it. In my opinion, it should be taken away." He also stated that "Sometimes, I think we give away too many datukships... it dilutes and devalues the award."[_[quote needs citation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Inline\_citation#When\_you\_must\_use\_inline\_citations "Wikipedia:Inline citation")_]

In the first government following the independence of Malaya in 1957, 5 of 15 cabinet Ministers were Datuks. The [Minister of Finance of Malaysia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minister_of_Finance_(Malaysia) "Minister of Finance (Malaysia)") at the time, [Tan Siew Sin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tan_Siew_Sin "Tan Siew Sin"), held the title [Justice of Peace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice_of_Peace "Justice of Peace"). Later, he was granted a Federal award which carried the title _Tun_. The father of Malayan independence, [Tunku Abdul Rahman Putra Al-Haj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunku_Abdul_Rahman "Tunku Abdul Rahman"), received no awards and carried the title _Tunku_, which he inherited as the prince of the state of Kedah. He was honorarily referred to as _Yang Teramat Mulia_ (YTM). The [Senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dewan_Negara "Dewan Negara") held only 14 _Datuk_ s, and [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dewan_Rakyat "Dewan Rakyat") held only seven.

The Malacca government was criticised for awarding the _Datuk_ title to a non-Malaysian Indian actor, [Shah Rukh Khan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shah_Rukh_Khan "Shah Rukh Khan"), for making movies and promoting the Malacca state internationally.[[21]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-44)

In 2024, the Yang di-Pertuan Agong, Sultan [Ibrahim Iskandar of Johor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibrahim_Iskandar_of_Johor "Ibrahim Iskandar of Johor"), stated "If a recipient is found to have tarnished the country’s image or commits a crime and has been punished by the court, then the award will be withdrawn. (Say) Goodbye to it." He also stated that "Accordingly, I have instructed the government to check the list of inmates in all prisons. I want any inmate with federal awards or honours to have their award withdrawn. This is only for federal awards and honours. As for awards conferred by other states, I do not interfere,".[[22]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-45)

### Johor

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=31 "Edit section: Johor")]

*   The Sultan of Johor revoked the titles of four _Dato'_ s in 2010.

### Malaysia

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=32 "Edit section: Malaysia")]

*   In 2026, the Yang di-Pertuan Agong revoked [Mohd Isa Abdul Samad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohd_Isa_Abdul_Samad "Mohd Isa Abdul Samad")'s Panglima Setia Mahkota award, which carries the “Tan Sri” title.[[23]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-46)

### Negeri Sembilan

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=33 "Edit section: Negeri Sembilan")]

*   In 2018, the Yang di-Pertuan Besar of Negeri Sembilan revoked the state honours bestowed upon [Mohammad Najib Abdul Razak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Najib_Razak "Najib Razak") and his wife, [Rosmah Mansor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosmah_Mansor "Rosmah Mansor").[[24]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-47)

### Pahang

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=34 "Edit section: Pahang")]

*   The Sultan of Pahang revoked the titles of two _Dato'_ s in 2004.

### Selangor

[[edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Malay_styles_and_titles&action=edit&section=35 "Edit section: Selangor")]

*   The _Dato'_ s of Selangor attempted to set up an association of Selangor Dato's. It received approval from the registrar of societies, but was shelved when the Sultan forbade any Dato' from joining or otherwise risk losing their title.
*   Four _Datuk_ s were removed in 2003 by the Sultan of Selangor.
*   _Dato' Seri_[Anwar Ibrahim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anwar_Ibrahim "Anwar Ibrahim") had his title revoked by the Sultan of Selangor on 3 November 2014.[[25]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-48)
*   In 2019, the Sultan of Selangor revoked the state honours bestowed upon [Najib Razak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Najib_Razak "Najib Razak") and his wife, [Rosmah Mansor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosmah_Mansor "Rosmah Mansor"), on 12 September 2022.[[26]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malay_styles_and_titles#cite_note-49)
