The Framework

## A compass for candid conversations

Radical Candor can be hard to put into practice in the heat of the moment. This framework helps you spot it.

*   ✓**Radical Candor**Care Personally and Challenge Directly at the same time.
*   ✗**Ruinous Empathy**You care, but you stay quiet or sugar-coat it.
*   ✗**Obnoxious Aggression**You challenge, but you don't show you care.
*   ✗**Manipulative Insincerity**Neither. Flattery up front, criticism behind their back.

These are behaviors everyone slips into sometimes, not personality types. Use it as a compass, not a label. Please don't write anyone's name in these boxes.

## Why is something so simple so hard to do?

![Image 4: Manager walking down the hallway with her team](https://www.radicalcandor.com/hs-fs/hubfs/our-approach-frame7/still_008.jpg?length=900&name=still_008.jpg)

### You're told you have to be professional

You've spent your whole career being told to stay professional, which too often means leaving your humanity at home. Real trust starts when you **Care Personally®**: showing enough of yourself that people feel safe doing the same.

![Image 5: Two colleagues, one holding back what he thinks](https://www.radicalcandor.com/hs-fs/hubfs/our-approach-frame7/still_030.jpg?length=900&name=still_030.jpg)

### “If you don't have anything nice to say…”

Since childhood you've heard some version of this. Then you become the boss, and the thing you've avoided since you were a toddler is suddenly the job. **Challenge Directly®** means sharing your honest opinion instead of staying quiet, because that's often the clearest way to show you care.
