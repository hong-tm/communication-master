## Enquiries - Complaint Steps v2

[](https://www.bnm.gov.my/contact-us/lodge-complaint)

**How to lodge a complaint?**

Three steps to follow when making a complaint:

STEP

1

Lodge your complaint with the Complaints Unit of your financial service providers

The list of their Complaints Unit can be found [here](https://www.bnm.gov.my/regulations/fsp-directory).

*   ![Image 9: Checkmark icon](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/check.svg) Contact the Complaints Unit as soon as possible. Please note that the Business Unit or Claims Unit is NOT the Complaints Unit.
*   ![Image 10: Checkmark icon](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/check.svg) BNM will not accept complaints that have not been referred to the Complaints Unit of the financial service providers.
*   ![Image 11: Checkmark icon](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/check.svg) If you wish to make a complaint on a BNM staff, you may do so in the [Whistleblowing](https://www.bnm.gov.my/whistleblowing-policy) page. If your complaint is related to scams, you may visit the [NSRC](https://nfcc.jpm.gov.my/index.php/en/about-nsrc) page.

STEP

2

Obtain a response and final decision on your complaint from the Complaints Unit

*   ![Image 12: Checkmark icon](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/check.svg) If you have not received any response within the timeframe provided by the Complaints Unit, you should contact them to follow up.
*   ![Image 13: Checkmark icon](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/check.svg) It is advisable to follow up in writing to obtain a proper record trail.

STEP

3

If you did not receive a response after 14 days:

*   ![Image 14: Checkmark icon](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/check.svg) You may refer your case to [BNMLINK](https://www.bnm.gov.my/contact-us/complaints#filtering).
*   OR
*   If you are dissatisfied with the decision of the financial service providers:
*   ![Image 15: Checkmark icon](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/check.svg)  You may refer to the[relevant redress channels](https://www.bnm.gov.my/contact-us/lodge-complaint#redress)to address your complaints.

Click [here](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/41144/Infographic_Redress_EN.pdf) to download the Redress Infographic in PDF format

## Enquiries Complaints - Scope

[](https://www.bnm.gov.my/contact-us/lodge-complaint)

**Scope of complaints handled**

Cases that fall outside of our jurisdiction

![Image 16](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/ec-cases.svg)

Cases that **have not been attended to** by the bank, insurer, takaful operator, and e-money issuer

You are advised to lodge a complaints with their Complaints Unit first. Please refer to the list of [Complaints Unit](https://www.bnm.gov.my/fsp-complaints-unit).

![Image 17](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/ec-regulators.svg)

Cases on **entities that are under the purview of other regulators or law enforcement agencies**

This includes [Securities Commission Malaysia](https://sc.com.my/) (SC), [Ministry of Domestic Trade and Cost of Living](https://www.kpdn.gov.my/) (KPDN), and [Royal Malaysian Police](https://rmp.gov.my/) (PDRM).

![Image 18](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/ec-law.svg)

Cases that have been **referred to the Financial Markets Ombudsman Service(FMOS), courts, or tribunals.**

For more information on FMOS, please visit [www.fmos.org.my](https://www.fmos.org.my/)

![Image 19](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/ec-bankruptcy-1.svg)

**Bankruptcy** issues

Please refer to the Malaysian Department of Insolvency: [www.mdi.gov.my](https://www.mdi.gov.my/)

![Image 20](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/ec-whistleblowing.svg)

**Whistleblowing** cases

Please refer to BNM's [Whistleblowing Policy](https://www.bnm.gov.my/whistleblowing-policy).

![Image 21](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/ec-anonymous.svg)

**Anonymous senders, ambiguous case, or unauthorised third parties**

![Image 22](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/ec-unclaimed.svg)

**Unclaimed monies** from bank accounts, insurance policies or takaful certificates

Please refer to the Unclaimed Monies Department under the Accountant-General's Office:[egumis.anm.gov.my](https://egumis.anm.gov.my/)

![Image 23](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/ec-agents.svg)

Cases by **any agents of a bank, insurer, takaful operator, and e-money issuer against their principal** (e.g. debt collector, car repossessor, auctioneer, insurance/takaful agents)

![Image 24](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/ec-staff-bank.svg)

Cases by **staff of a bank, insurer, takaful operator, and e-money issuer** against their principals or employers

![Image 25](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/ec-timeout.svg)

**Time-barred** cases (according to Limitation Act 1953 or Limitation Ordinance (Sabah) (Cap.72), or Limitation Ordinance (Sarawak) (Cap.49))

![Image 26](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/ec-commercial.svg)

Cases related to bank, insurer, takaful operator, and e-money issuer's **commercial decisions**

![Image 27](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/ec-law.svg)

Requests for **interpretation of any Act, contract terms, policy, or contractual wording.** BNMLINK does not provide any legal opinions for the complainant

Important: Credit companies, leasing and factoring companies, pawn brokers and credit community companies are not regulated by BNM.

## Complaints - Redress channels

[](https://www.bnm.gov.my/contact-us/lodge-complaint)

**Alternative redress channels**

![Image 28](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/41144/akpk_logo.png)
[Agensi Kaunseling dan Pengurusan Kredit (AKPK)](http://www.akpk.org.my/)

Set up by BNM to help individuals/ SMEs take control of their financial situation and gain peace of mind arising from wise use of credit

![Image 29](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3867047/fmos_logo.png)
[Financial Markets Ombudsman Service (FMOS)](http://www.fmos.org.my/)

Established by BNM and SC to provide an independent, fair, and free dispute resolution service to financial consumers and investors, who are unable to resolve their complaints with financial service providers and capital market intermediaries

![Image 30](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3866789/justice-scale.jpg?s=1)
Legal Assistance

As an alternative next step, financial consumers may also seek legal assistance to resolve their situation.

![Image 31](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/3867047/ttpm-logo.jpg)
Tribunal for Consumer Claims

Please visit the [Ministry of Domestic Trade's Tribunal website](https://ttpm.kpdn.gov.my/portal/home) for more information.

![Image 32](https://www.bnm.gov.my/documents/20124/41144/cdrc_logo.jpg)
[Corporate Debt Restructuring Committee (CDRC)](https://www.cdrc.my/)

Platform for corporate borrowers and their creditors to work out feasible debt resolutions without having to resort to legal proceedings

This initiative has been put in place to ensure that all avenues are made available to assist distressed corporations in resolving their debt obligations
