# Types of deceptive pattern

A taxonomy mapped to laws and enforcement actions.

## [Sneaking → The user is drawn into a transaction on false pretences, because pertinent information is hidden or delayed from being presented to them.](https://deceptive.design/types/sneaking/)

[33 laws & rules](https://deceptive.design/laws/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Sneaking)[60 enforcements](https://deceptive.design/enforcement/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Sneaking)[18 examples](https://deceptive.design/hall-of-shame/browse/?f_Type=Sneaking)

## [Forced action → The user wants to do something, but they are required to do something else undesirable in return.](https://deceptive.design/types/forced-action/)

[41 laws & rules](https://deceptive.design/laws/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Forced+action)[59 enforcements](https://deceptive.design/enforcement/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Forced+action)[23 examples](https://deceptive.design/hall-of-shame/browse/?f_Type=Forced+action)

## [Hard to cancel → The user finds it easy to sign up or subscribe, but when they want to cancel they find it very hard.](https://deceptive.design/types/hard-to-cancel/)

[46 laws & rules](https://deceptive.design/laws/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Hard+to+cancel)[23 enforcements](https://deceptive.design/enforcement/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Hard+to+cancel)[135 examples](https://deceptive.design/hall-of-shame/browse/?f_Type=Hard+to+cancel)

## [Preselection → The user is presented with a default option that has already been selected for them, in order to influence their decision-making.](https://deceptive.design/types/preselection/)

[22 laws & rules](https://deceptive.design/laws/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Preselection)[14 enforcements](https://deceptive.design/enforcement/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Preselection)[85 examples](https://deceptive.design/hall-of-shame/browse/?f_Type=Preselection)

## [Obstruction → The user is faced with barriers or hurdles, making it hard for them to complete their task or access information.](https://deceptive.design/types/obstruction/)

[14 laws & rules](https://deceptive.design/laws/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Obstruction)[12 enforcements](https://deceptive.design/enforcement/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Obstruction)[21 examples](https://deceptive.design/hall-of-shame/browse/?f_Type=Obstruction)

## [Hidden subscription → The user is unknowingly enrolled in a recurring subscription or payment plan without clear disclosure or their explicit consent.](https://deceptive.design/types/hidden-subscription/)

[22 laws & rules](https://deceptive.design/laws/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Hidden+subscription)[11 enforcements](https://deceptive.design/enforcement/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Hidden+subscription)[28 examples](https://deceptive.design/hall-of-shame/browse/?f_Type=Hidden+subscription)

## [Hidden costs → The user is enticed with a low advertised price. After investing time and effort, they discover unexpected fees and charges when they reach the checkout.](https://deceptive.design/types/hidden-costs/)

[17 laws & rules](https://deceptive.design/laws/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Hidden+costs)[10 enforcements](https://deceptive.design/enforcement/browse/?f_Deceptive+pattern=Hidden+costs)[15 examples](https://deceptive.design/hall-of-shame/browse/?f_Type=Hidden+Costs)

## [Trick wording → The user is misled into taking an action, due to the presentation of confusing or misleading language.](https://deceptive.design/types/trick-wording/)

## [Visual interference → The user expects to see information presented in a clear and predictable way on the page, but it is hidden, obscured or disguised.](https://deceptive.design/types/visual-interference/)

## [Nagging → The user tries to do something, but they are persistently interrupted by requests to do something else that may not be in their best interests.](https://deceptive.design/types/nagging/)
